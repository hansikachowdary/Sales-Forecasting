03 / INTERPRETATION

Understand the signal.

Forecasting starts with knowing what the series is doing before asking it to predict.

A

Trend

Long-term movement in the level of sales. The rolling mean smooths short-term noise to make it visible.

B

Seasonality

Repeating patterns at fixed intervals. SARIMA can model the 12-month rhythm of monthly data.

C

Stationarity

ARIMA differences the series to stabilize its mean before learning temporal relationships.

D

Autocorrelation

Each observation carries information about its neighbors. Time order is preserved throughout validation.

How the winner is chosen
80% of the timeline trains each model. The final 20% is held back as unseen test data. The model with the lowest RMSE becomes the default.
